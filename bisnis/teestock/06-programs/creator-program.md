# TeeStock Creator Program v1.0

> **Canonical TeeStock Creator Participation Framework**  
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan creator eligibility, artwork submission, licensing, review, royalty, co-drop, creator merchandising, attribution, payout, lifecycle, tiers, performance review, IP governance, automation, metrics, dan boundaries untuk TeeStock Creator Program.

---

# 1. Purpose

Creator Program menjawab:

> **Bagaimana artist, designer, illustrator, creator, dan pemilik audience dapat berpartisipasi dalam ecosystem TeeStock melalui creative contribution dan audience collaboration dengan economics yang jelas?**

Canonical principle:

> **Create with TeeStock. Grow with TeeStock.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Creator Program adalah structured participation program yang memungkinkan creators berkontribusi melalui artwork, creative IP, audience, content, atau collaborative product development untuk memperoleh royalty, revenue participation, commercial exposure, atau progression menuju recurring merchandise partnership.**

---

# 183. Creator Program Failure Modes

## Accept Everyone

Destroys curation.

## Rights After Launch

Creates avoidable IP risk.

## Royalty by Spreadsheet Guess

Creates distrust.

## Follower Count = Quality

Bad qualification.

## No Artwork Versioning

Production/IP confusion.

## Creator Program = Merch Service

Architectural confusion.

## No Exit Process

Products remain live after rights end.

## Too Many Active Creators

Operational overload.

---

# 184. What Creator Program Must Not Become

## Open Artwork Marketplace

Curation remains core.

## Speculative Artist Exploitation

Commercial rights and compensation must be explicit.

## Influencer Agency

Not core scope.

## Free Creative Labor Pool

Accepted commercial value requires agreed economics.

## Program Without Rights Tracking

IP is foundational.
