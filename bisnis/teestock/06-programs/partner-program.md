# TeeStock Partner Program v1.0

> **Canonical TeeStock Operational Partner Network Framework**  
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan partner types, qualification, capability registry, rate cards, SLA, quality governance, work-order routing, capacity, confidentiality, scorecards, preferred/backup status, incident handling, economics, automation, dan MGBOS integration untuk TeeStock Partner Program.

---

# 1. Purpose

Partner Program menjawab:

> **Bagaimana TeeStock memperluas operational capability tanpa harus memiliki semua mesin, fasilitas, supplier, warehouse, dan specialist skill sendiri?**

Canonical principle:

> **Partner for capability. Own the outcome.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Partner Program adalah structured operational network yang menghubungkan TeeStock dengan qualified external businesses atau specialists yang menyediakan production, sourcing, supply, logistics, fulfillment, dan other approved capabilities melalui defined standards, commercial terms, capacity visibility, quality controls, dan performance governance.**

# 4. Core Philosophy

Canonical:

```text id="ptn002"
OWN
what creates strategic advantage.

PARTNER
what can be reliably externalized.

AUTOMATE

```

Partner Program operationalizes the second principle.

---

# 5. Partner Program Is Not Supply

Critical distinction:

```text id="ptn003"
TEEStock SUPPLY
TeeStock sells product inputs to customers.

PARTNER PROGRAM
external parties provide capability to TeeStock.
```

---

# 6. Partner Program Is Not Vendor List

A spreadsheet of supplier phone numbers is not a Partner Program.

A real Partner Program knows:

```text id="ptn004"

CAN DO WHAT
AT WHAT QUALITY
AT WHAT COST
WITH WHAT CAPACITY
UNDER WHAT TERMS
```

# 7. Partner Program Is Not Outsourcing Responsibility

Canonical:

> **TeeStock may outsource execution. It does not outsource accountability for the promise TeeStock made to its customer.**

# 202. Partner Program Failure Modes

## Cheapest Vendor Wins Everything

Quality/SLA collapse.

## Partner List Without Capability Data

No useful routing.

## No Backup for Critical Capability

Single-point failure.

## Work Orders in Chat Only

No traceability.

## No QC Attribution

Cannot improve Partner performance.

## No Rate Versioning

Commercial disputes.

## Partner Owns Customer Outcome

TeeStock loses control.

## Too Many Vendors

Network becomes unmanaged.

# 203. What Partner Program Must Not Become

## Open Vendor Marketplace

Qualification remains deliberate.

## Race-to-the-Bottom Procurement System

Total operating outcome matters.

## Responsibility Escape Mechanism

TeeStock owns customer promise.

## Informal Founder Contact List

Relationships become system assets.

## Partner Dependency Trap

Critical capabilities require risk awareness.
