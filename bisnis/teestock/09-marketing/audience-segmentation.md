# 1. Purpose

Audience Segmentation menjawab:

> **Siapa yang TeeStock layani, problem apa yang sedang mereka pecahkan, seberapa kuat intent mereka, bagaimana mereka membeli, dan pengalaman apa yang seharusnya TeeStock berikan kepada masing-masing kelompok?**

Canonical principle:

> **Segment by meaningful differences in customer need and behavior, not by demographic decoration.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Audience Segmentation adalah system untuk mengelompokkan prospects, customers, accounts, creators, resellers, affiliates, dan partners berdasarkan shared problems, intent, behavior, lifecycle, commercial value, dan operational requirements sehingga offer, message, channel, experience, pricing, dan automation dapat disesuaikan secara relevan.**

---

# 3. Segmentation Is Not Persona Theater

Critical distinction:

```text id="seg001"
SEGMENT
group with meaningful commercial/behavioral similarity.

PERSONA
narrative representation of a segment.
```

Persona hanya berguna jika berdiri di atas real segment.

---

# 4. Demographics Are Context

Tetapi tidak cukup untuk menjelaskan:

```text id="seg002"

```

# 272. Segmentation Failure Modes

## Age = Segment

Too shallow.

## Everyone = Customer

No focus.

## Persona Without Data

Fiction.

## High Revenue = Best Customer

Ignores contribution.

## Followers = Best Creator

Ignores audience quality.

## Segment With No Different Action

Unnecessary complexity.

## Static Lifecycle

Misses changing intent.

## Separate Identity for Every Role

Data fragmentation.

# 273. What Segmentation Must Not Become

## Demographic Stereotyping

Needs and behavior matter more.

## CRM Tag Graveyard

Every tag must have operational purpose.

## Hyper-Personalization Theater

Relevance before complexity.

## AI Profiling Machine

Use transparent, business-relevant signals.

## Segment Prison

Customers can change behavior and needs.
