# 1. Purpose

Go-To-Market Strategy menjawab:

> **Bagaimana TeeStock masuk ke pasar, menarik customer pertama, membangun demand yang repeatable, dan memperbesar distribusi tanpa membakar modal sebelum product-market signal terbukti?**

Canonical principle:

> **Earn demand before scaling distribution.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Go-To-Market adalah coordinated market-entry system yang menghubungkan audience, problem, offer, message, channel, conversion path, fulfillment capability, unit economics, dan feedback loops untuk menghasilkan demand yang measurable dan scalable.**

---

# 3. GTM Is Not Marketing Calendar

Critical distinction:

```text id="gtm001"
MARKETING CAMPAIGN
is a tactic.

GO-TO-MARKET
is the system connecting
market → offer → channel → conversion → economics.
```
