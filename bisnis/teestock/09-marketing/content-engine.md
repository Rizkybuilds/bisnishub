# TeeStock Content Engine v1.0

> **Canonical TeeStock Content Strategy, Production & Distribution Framework**  
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan bagaimana TeeStock mengubah audience insight, products, services, brand ideas, customer proof, creator participation, market questions, dan operational knowledge menjadi repeatable content yang membangun awareness, trust, demand, conversion, retention, dan market intelligence.

---

# 1. Purpose

Content Engine menjawab:

> **Apa yang harus TeeStock bicarakan, kepada siapa, dalam format apa, untuk tujuan apa, dan bagaimana konten tersebut diproduksi secara konsisten tanpa menjadi random posting machine?**

Canonical principle:

> **Content should move the audience, not merely fill the calendar.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Content Engine adalah operating system yang mengubah audience needs, commercial priorities, product stories, market signals, creator participation, dan internal expertise menjadi planned, produced, distributed, measured, repurposed, dan archived content assets yang mendukung brand building serta measurable demand creation.**

# 260. Content Failure Modes

## Random Posting

No strategy.

## One Content for Everyone

Weak relevance.

## Chasing Trends

Weak brand memory.

## Views = Success

No commercial context.

## No Content Rights

Legal/IP risk.

## No Product Fact Review

Misinformation.

## Posting More to Fix Weak Content

Waste.

## AI Generates Everything

Generic brand.

## Content Factory

Volume is not objective.

## Meme Account

Unless strategically relevant.

## Sales Catalogue Only

Brand and education matter.

## Brand Film Studio Only

Demand capture matters.

## AI Slop Machine

Automation must improve leverage without destroying quality.
