# TeeStock Retention & Community v1.0

> **Canonical TeeStock Customer Lifecycle, Retention, Loyalty, Referral & Community Framework**  
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan post-purchase lifecycle, repeat purchase, reorder, customer lifecycle, retention triggers, loyalty logic, review loops, referrals, win-back, lapse/churn, community participation, creator/community flywheels, relationship economics, lifecycle automation, CRM orchestration, dan MGBOS integration.

---

# 1. Purpose

Retention & Community menjawab:

> **Bagaimana TeeStock mengubah satu transaksi menjadi hubungan yang berulang, relevan, profitable, dan semakin kuat dari waktu ke waktu?**

Canonical principle:

> **Acquisition starts the relationship. Retention compounds it.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Retention & Community adalah lifecycle system yang mengubah first-time customers, business accounts, creators, communities, dan other participants menjadi repeat relationships melalui strong customer experience, relevant communication, reorder convenience, product relevance, recognition, participation, referral, and structured community engagement.**

---

# 3. Retention Is Not Discounting

Critical distinction:

```text id="retc001"
RETENTION
creates reasons to return.

DISCOUNTING
reduces price.
```

Discount can support retention.

It should not become the primary reason customers stay.

# 279. Retention Failure Modes

## Acquire and Forget

CAC repeats forever.

## Discount = Loyalty

Weak relationship.

## Ask for Review Too Early

Poor experience.

## Spam Every Customer

Destroys relevance.

## B2B Reorder from Scratch

Unnecessary friction.

## Loyalty Points Before Repeat Behavior

Complexity without value.

## Followers = Community

False signal.

## Win-Back = Coupon

Margin leakage.

---

# 280. What Retention & Community Must Not Become

## Discount Dependency

Customers should return for value.

## CRM Spam Engine

Relationship requires relevance.

## Gamification Theater

Points/badges are not loyalty.

## Forced Community

Not every customer wants participation.

## AI Manipulation Engine

Automation should improve timing and relevance, not exploit people.
