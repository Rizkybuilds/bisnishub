# 1. Purpose

Channel Strategy menjawab:

> **Melalui channel mana TeeStock harus ditemukan, dipercaya, dibeli, dihubungi, dan diingat—serta apa fungsi masing-masing channel dalam customer journey dan economics TeeStock?**

Canonical principle:

> **Every channel needs a job.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Channel Strategy adalah portfolio framework yang menetapkan fungsi setiap acquisition, distribution, communication, and sales channel berdasarkan audience, intent, customer journey, economics, data ownership, operational complexity, dan strategic control sehingga TeeStock dapat memperluas demand tanpa menciptakan channel chaos.**

---

# 3. Channel Is Not Strategy

Critical distinction:

```text
CHANNEL
is where interaction happens.

STRATEGY
defines why that channel exists.
```

# 7. Owned vs Rented Distribution

Canonical:

```text
OWNED
TeeStock controls customer relationship/data more directly.

RENTED
platform controls significant parts of reach, rules, and access.
```

# 235. Channel Failure Modes

## Every Channel Has Same Job

No strategic clarity.

## Marketplace Revenue = Success

Ignores margin.

## Social Followers = Growth

Ignores conversion.

## WhatsApp = CRM

Data chaos.

## Paid Ads Before Offer Validation

Cash waste.

## Creator = Follower Count

Poor distribution quality.

## Reseller Without Price Governance

Channel conflict.

## Email Without Segmentation

Spam.

## Platform-Chasing

Follow customer behavior, not platform hype.

## Channel Proliferation

Every channel creates operating cost.

Customer journeys are more complex.

Owned/partner distribution matters.

## Channel Silos

Customer should experience one TeeStock.
