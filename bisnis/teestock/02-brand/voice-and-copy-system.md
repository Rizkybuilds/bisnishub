# TeeStock Voice & Copy System v1.0

> **Canonical Verbal Identity Framework**  
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan bagaimana TeeStock berbicara, menulis, menjelaskan product, menawarkan service, memberikan instruction, menangani customer, dan membangun trust di seluruh touchpoint.

---

# 1. Purpose

Voice & Copy System menjawab:

- bagaimana TeeStock terdengar,
- kata apa yang digunakan,
- kata apa yang dihindari,
- bagaimana tone berubah berdasarkan audience,
- bagaimana product dijelaskan,
- bagaimana CTA ditulis,
- bagaimana harga dan kualitas dikomunikasikan,
- bagaimana customer service berbicara,
- bagaimana promo dibuat tanpa merusak brand,
- dan bagaimana Independent Labels dapat memiliki voice sendiri tanpa merusak relationship dengan TeeStock.

Core principle:

> **Clear enough to understand. Human enough to trust. Confident enough to choose.**

---

# 2. Voice vs Tone

## Voice

Voice adalah personality verbal TeeStock yang relatif stabil.

Canonical voice:

```text
CLEAR
WARM
DIRECT
INFORMED
CALM
PRACTICAL
```

---

## Tone

Tone menyesuaikan context.

Contoh:

```text
Product Page
→ confident + descriptive

Custom
→ helpful + practical

Business
→ professional + efficient

Customer Support
→ calm + solution-oriented

Originals
→ expressive + cultural
```

Voice tetap sama.

Tone yang berubah.

---

# 3. Core Voice Statement

> **TeeStock berbicara seperti orang yang benar-benar mengerti apparel, tetapi cukup percaya diri untuk menjelaskannya secara sederhana.**

TeeStock tidak mencoba terdengar:

- paling pintar,
- paling premium,
- paling keren,
- atau paling murah.

TeeStock berusaha terdengar:

> **jelas dan dapat dipercaya.**

---

# 4. Verbal Personality

TeeStock adalah:

```text
KNOWLEDGEABLE
but not condescending

FRIENDLY
but not childish

CONFIDENT
but not arrogant

MODERN
but not slang-dependent

TECHNICAL
but not confusing

COMMERCIAL
but not pushy
```

---

# 5. Primary Communication Principle

Canonical rule:

> **Explain before persuade.**

Customer seharusnya memahami:

- apa produknya,
- siapa cocok menggunakannya,
- apa bedanya,
- apa yang akan terjadi berikutnya

sebelum dipaksa membeli.

---

# 6. Secondary Communication Principle

> **Proof before adjectives.**

Kurangi:

```text
premium
super quality
best
amazing
exclusive
```

Perbanyak:

```text
material
fit
construction
process
testing
warranty
delivery expectation
```

---

# 7. Example: Weak vs Strong Copy

Weak:

> Kaos premium dengan kualitas terbaik.

Preferred:

> Heavyweight cotton dengan feel lebih padat, potongan clean, dan collar yang dibuat untuk tetap rapi dipakai berulang kali.

Alasannya:

Preferred copy menjelaskan **kenapa** product bernilai.

---

# 8. Language System

TeeStock dapat menggunakan:

```text
Bahasa Indonesia
+
natural English terms
```

jika istilah English memang sudah umum atau lebih ringkas.

Contoh acceptable:

- fit,
- heavyweight,
- custom,
- drop,
- creator,
- mockup,
- fulfillment,
- oversized.

Jangan memaksakan English jika Bahasa Indonesia lebih jelas.

---

# 9. Indonesian Style

Gunakan Bahasa Indonesia yang:

- natural,
- contemporary,
- conversational,
- tidak terlalu formal.

Avoid:

> Dengan hormat kami informasikan bahwasanya...

Preferred:

> Pesanan lo sudah masuk dan sekarang sedang kami siapkan.

Untuk konteks B2B formal, level formalitas dapat dinaikkan.

---

# 10. Pronoun System

Default consumer-facing language dapat menggunakan:

```text
lo
```

jika brand implementation memutuskan personality yang casual.

Namun secara system, TeeStock harus mampu menggunakan dua mode:

```text
CASUAL
lo / kamu

PROFESSIONAL
Anda / pihak perusahaan
```

Jangan mencampur kedua mode dalam satu experience.

---

# 11. Recommended Usage

## Consumer

Prefer:

```text
lo
```

jika consistent dengan active brand execution.

## Creator

Prefer:

```text
lo
```

atau nama creator.

## B2B

Prefer:

```text
Anda
tim Anda
perusahaan Anda
```

atau neutral phrasing.

---

# 12. Sentence Style

Prefer:

- short to medium sentences,
- active voice,
- concrete nouns,
- specific verbs.

Avoid:

- long corporate constructions,
- filler,
- excessive modifiers.

---

# 13. Copy Structure

Recommended:

```text
CLAIM
↓
EXPLANATION
↓
PROOF
↓
ACTION
```

Example:

> **Built for everyday wear.**  
> Cotton yang terasa lebih substantial dengan fit yang tetap gampang dipakai.  
> Tersedia dalam beberapa ukuran dan warna inti.  
> **Pilih warna lo.**

---

# 14. Message Hierarchy

Setiap page harus mempunyai:

```text
ONE PRIMARY MESSAGE
ONE PRIMARY CTA
SUPPORTING PROOF
```

Jangan membuat semua hal terlihat sama penting.

Avoid:

```text
termurah
terbaik se-Indonesia
nomor satu
auto keren
wajib punya
auto cuan
100% pasti
viral
sultan
murah banget
```

kecuali campaign context memang sengaja menggunakan language tersebut dan tetap credible.

---

# 18. Quality Language

Do not say:

> premium quality

without context.

Instead explain:

```text
fabric
weight
fit
construction
print process
QC
```

---

# 19. Accessibility Language

TeeStock tidak berbicara seperti gatekeeper fashion.

Avoid:

> Kalau ngerti fashion, pasti tahu...

Preferred:

> Kalau lo suka tee dengan feel lebih tebal dan struktur lebih clean, ini pilihannya.

---

# 20. Expertise Language

Expertise terlihat dari clarity.

Avoid:

> Kami adalah ahli dengan pengalaman luar biasa...

Preferred:

> Untuk artwork dengan detail kecil, kami akan cek ukuran dan metode produksinya dulu sebelum masuk produksi.

---

# 21. Commerce Voice

Commerce tone:

```text
DESCRIPTIVE
CONFIDENT
LIGHT
USEFUL
```

Customer harus cepat memahami product.

---

# 22. Product Name Rule

Product name harus:

- recognizable,
- searchable,
- easy to remember.

Recommended:

```text
Heavyweight Essential Tee
Oversized Essential Tee
Start Anyway Tee
```

Avoid nama abstrak jika customer tidak tahu produknya.

---

# 23. Product Description Framework

Canonical sequence:

```text
1. WHAT IT IS
2. WHY IT EXISTS
3. HOW IT FEELS / FITS
4. MATERIAL / CONSTRUCTION
5. WHO IT SUITS
6. CARE / DELIVERY
```

---

# 24. Product Copy Example Structure

```text
[Product Name]

One-line proposition.

Why you'll like it:
- benefit
- benefit
- benefit

Material:
...

Fit:
...

Care:
...
```

Product copy tidak harus menjadi essay.

---

# 25. Feature → Benefit Rule

Never stop at specification.

Example:

```text
Feature:
Heavyweight fabric

Benefit:
Feels more structured and holds shape better.
```

---

# 26. TeeStock Selects Voice

Selects should sound:

```text
CURATED
VISUAL
CONCISE
```

Key framing:

> Dipilih TeeStock karena desainnya punya alasan untuk ada, bukan sekadar menambah katalog.

Avoid implying:

> TeeStock created every artwork.

---

# 27. TeeStock Essentials Voice

Essentials should sound:

```text
SIMPLE
FUNCTIONAL
EVERGREEN
```

Focus:

- material,
- fit,
- comfort,
- repeat wear.

Less storytelling.

More product clarity.

---

# 28. TeeStock Custom Voice

Custom tone:

```text

REASSURING
PRACTICAL
```

Core message:

> Lo bawa ide. Kita bantu jadi apparel.

Customer should feel:

> gue tidak harus ngerti produksi dulu.

---

# 29. Custom Copy Structure

Recommended:

```text
WHAT YOU WANT
↓
WHAT WE NEED
↓
WHAT WE DO
↓
WHAT IT COSTS
↓
HOW LONG
```

---

# 30. Custom CTA

Preferred:

```text
Mulai Custom Order
Upload Desain

```

Avoid generic:

```text
Pelajari Selengkapnya
```

---

# 31. TeeStock Business Voice

Business tone:

```text
PROFESSIONAL
CLEAR
EFFICIENT
```

Less casual slang.

Prioritize:

- requirement,
- timeline,
- quantity,
- specification,
- quotation,
- accountability.

---

# 32. Business Messaging

Core message:

> Apparel dan merchandise bisnis tanpa koordinasi vendor yang berantakan.

Supporting message:

> Satu partner untuk product, production, dan fulfillment sesuai kebutuhan proyek.

---

# 33. Business CTA

Preferred:

```text
Request Quote
Diskusikan Kebutuhan
Kirim Brief
Buat Estimasi
```

---

# 34. TeeStock Merch Voice

Merch tone:

```text
COLLABORATIVE
CREATOR-FOCUSED
CAPABLE
```

Creator adalah partner.

Jangan berbicara seolah TeeStock “menolong creator kecil”.

Preferred:

> Lo fokus ke audience dan creative direction. TeeStock bantu product, production, dan fulfillment-nya.

---

# 35. Merch Messaging

Avoid:

> Kami urus semuanya, Anda tinggal santai.

Preferred:

> Kita bagi fokus: lo pegang audience dan direction, TeeStock mengoperasikan backend merch.

---

# 36. TeeStock Studio Voice

Studio:

```text
CREATIVE
STRATEGIC
CLEAR
```

Avoid overly agency-like phrases:

> We create disruptive visual experiences.

Preferred:

> Dari graphic sampai packaging, Studio membantu mengubah ide menjadi product system yang siap diproduksi.

---

# 37. TeeStock Supply Voice

Supply tone:

```text
TECHNICAL
DIRECT
RELIABLE
```

Prioritize:

- product specification,
- availability,
- MOQ,
- lead time,
- pricing tier.

Minimal fluff.

---

# 38. TeeStock Fulfill Voice

Fulfill tone:

```text
OPERATIONAL
CALM
PRECISE
```

Core message:

> Barang masuk. Order diproses. Customer menerima.

Proof matters:

- accuracy,
- SLA,
- tracking,
- reporting.

---

# 39. Creator Program Voice

Tone:

```text
RESPECTFUL
OPEN
COLLABORATIVE
```

Avoid:

> Kirim desainmu dan dapatkan cuan!

Preferred:

> Punya artwork atau audience? Kita bisa eksplorasi cara mengubahnya menjadi apparel yang layak dijual.

---

# 40. Reseller Program Voice

Focus on:

- margin,
- process,
- catalog,
- fulfillment.

Avoid get-rich-quick framing.

Preferred:

> Jual produk TeeStock tanpa harus menangani produksi sendiri.

---

# 41. Affiliate Program Voice

Be transparent about:

- attribution,
- commission,
- payout conditions.

Avoid income promises.

---

# 42. Originals Voice

TeeStock Originals boleh lebih expressive.

Tone:

```text
EDITORIAL
CULTURAL
IDEA-DRIVEN
```

Master brand says:

> what it does.

Originals may say:

> what it believes.

---

# 43. Originals Copy Principle

Originals should not sound like a generic apparel shop.

It may use:

- manifesto,
- short statements,
- concepts,
- cultural observations.

But avoid empty pseudo-philosophy.

---

# 44. Independent Label Voice

Each Label must define:

```text
BELIEF
AUDIENCE
VOICE
VOCABULARY
TABOOS
RHYTHM
```

Label voice may differ significantly from TeeStock.

Example:

```text
DO YOUR BEST
encouraging
disciplined
grounded

WORK IN PROGRESS
reflective
unfinished
human

AFTER HOURS CLUB
insider
quiet
builder-oriented
```

---

# 45. TeeStock Endorsement Copy

Recommended:

```text
A TeeStock Original
```

or:

```text
Created by TeeStock Originals
```

Do not over-explain ownership in consumer-facing copy unless relevant.

---

# 46. CTA System

CTA should describe the next action.

Use verbs.

Preferred:

```text
Shop Selects
Shop Essentials
Start Custom Order
Request Quote
Build Your Merch
Join Creator Program
View Collection
Choose Size
Add to Cart
Track Order
```

---

# 47. CTA Hierarchy

## Primary CTA

Main next action.

## Secondary CTA

Alternative path.

## Tertiary CTA

Low-commitment informational action.

Do not show five equal CTAs.

---

# 48. Promotional Copy

Promotion must remain truthful.

Preferred:

```text
20% off this weekend
```

if real.

Avoid artificial:

```text
LAST CHANCE!!!
HURRY!!!
ONLY TODAY!!!
```

if campaign repeats constantly.

---

# 49. Urgency Rule

Urgency must be tied to something factual:

- stock,
- date,
- drop window,
- preorder closing.

Not manufactured pressure.

---

# 50. Scarcity Rule

If limited:

state exactly what is limited.

Example:

> Chapter 001 diproduksi dalam batch terbatas untuk release pertama.

Better than:

> Super exclusive limited edition.

---

# 51. Pricing Copy

Price should be visible and easy to understand.

For retail:

```text
Rp129.000
```

For custom/service:

```text
Mulai dari Rp...
```

only if base assumptions are explained.

---

# 52. Price Transparency

Avoid:

> mulai Rp50.000

if realistic order usually costs Rp200.000.

Entry price should represent an actual purchase scenario.

---

# 53. Discount Copy

Preferred:

```text
Launch Price
Bundle Price
Member Price
Volume Price
```

Avoid constant:

```text
NORMAL 299K
NOW 99K
```

if normal price is not genuinely used.

---

# 54. Trust Copy

Trust statements should connect to proof.

Example:

Weak:

> Dijamin berkualitas.

Better:

> Setiap print melewati QC sebelum packing.

---

# 55. Delivery Copy

Be specific.

Example:

> Diproses dalam 1–3 hari kerja sebelum dikirim.

Do not say:

> Fast shipping

if meaning is unclear.

---

# 56. Stock Copy

Preferred status:

```text
Ready
Low Stock
Made to Order
Preorder
Out of Stock
```

Avoid fake inventory countdown.

---

# 57. Returns Copy

Return language should be:

```text
CLEAR
FAIR
NON-DEFENSIVE
```

Preferred:

> Ada masalah pada produk yang diterima? Kirim foto dan nomor order, tim TeeStock akan cek solusinya.

---

# 58. Error Message Voice

Error messages must explain:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED
+
WHAT TO DO
```

Weak:

> Error 403.

Better:

> File belum bisa di-upload. Coba gunakan JPG, PNG, atau PDF di bawah batas ukuran yang tersedia.

---

# 59. Empty State Copy

Empty state should guide action.

Example:

> Belum ada produk di wishlist lo.  
> Mulai dari Selects atau Essentials.

---

# 60. Form Copy

Form labels should be direct.

Preferred:

```text
Nama
Email
Jumlah
Jenis Produk
Deadline
Upload Desain
Catatan
```

Avoid clever naming.

---

# 61. Form Helper Text

Use helper text only when needed.

Example:

> Belum punya artwork final? Upload referensi juga boleh.

This reduces customer anxiety.

---

# 62. Confirmation Copy

After transaction, tell customer what happens next.

Example:

> Order berhasil dibuat. Setelah pembayaran terverifikasi, TeeStock mulai menyiapkan pesanan lo.

---

# 63. Status Copy

Canonical status language should be customer-understandable.

Internal:

```text
PRODUCTION_PENDING
```

Customer-facing:

```text
Menunggu Produksi
```

Internal technical language should not leak unnecessarily.

---

# 64. Customer Support Voice

Canonical tone:

```text
CALM
RESPECTFUL
ACCOUNTABLE
SOLUTION-ORIENTED
```

Never blame customer first.

---

# 65. Complaint Handling Structure

Recommended:

```text
ACKNOWLEDGE
↓
CLARIFY
↓
EXPLAIN
↓
SOLVE
↓
CONFIRM
```

---

# 66. Complaint Copy Example

Preferred structure:

> Makasih sudah kirim fotonya. Dari gambar ini, bagian print memang terlihat tidak sesuai standar TeeStock. Tim kami akan proses penggantian dan mengirim update berikutnya setelah order replacement dibuat.

Avoid:

> Maaf atas ketidaknyamanannya, mungkin terjadi karena ekspedisi.

unless verified.

---

# 67. Delay Communication

Do not hide delay.

Preferred:

> Produksi order lo mundur satu hari karena item ukuran L perlu restock. Estimasi baru dikirim pada [tanggal].

Specificity builds trust.

---

# 68. Apology Rule

Apologize when TeeStock caused inconvenience.

Do not over-apologize in routine communication.

Use:

```text
acknowledgment
+
accountability
+
solution
```

---

# 69. WhatsApp / Chat Tone

Chat can be warmer and shorter.

Example:

> Halo, desainnya sudah masuk. Untuk ukuran print ini masih aman. Kita tinggal butuh pilihan warna kaos dan jumlah per ukuran sebelum lanjut estimasi.

Avoid sending:

- huge paragraphs,
- overly formal scripts.

---

# 70. Email Tone

Email may be slightly more formal.

Structure:

```text
CONTEXT
KEY INFORMATION
ACTION
NEXT STEP
```

No unnecessary corporate filler.

---

# 71. Notification Copy

Notifications should be short.

Examples:

```text
Pembayaran berhasil.
Order masuk produksi.
Pesanan sudah dikirim.
Royalty creator sudah diproses.
```

---

# 72. B2B Proposal Copy

B2B proposal should prioritize:

- objective,
- specification,
- scope,
- price,
- timeline,
- terms.

Avoid long brand storytelling before customer sees solution.

---

# 73. Quotation Language

Quotation should be factual.

Include:

- item,
- quantity,
- unit price,
- subtotal,
- lead time,
- validity,
- payment terms.

No ambiguity.

---

# 74. Product Education Content

Educational content should answer real questions:

```text
24s vs 30s?
DTF vs screen printing?
Oversized fit means what?
How should I wash printed apparel?
```

This reinforces Guide archetype.

---

# 75. Marketing Content Voice

Marketing can be more energetic than core UX.

But still follow:

```text
REAL
USEFUL
SPECIFIC
```

Do not turn TeeStock into hype language on social while website sounds calm.

---

# 76. Content Hook Rule

Hooks can create curiosity.

They should not mislead.

Good:

> Kenapa dua kaos yang sama-sama cotton bisa terasa sangat beda?

Poor:

> Industri kaos selama ini membohongi lo.

unless evidence truly supports it.

---

# 77. Social Caption Structure

Recommended:

```text
HOOK
↓
POINT
↓
PROOF / CONTEXT
↓
CTA
```

Captions do not need to restate everything in the visual.

---

# 78. SEO Copy Rule

SEO keywords must not make copy robotic.

Write for humans first.

Use relevant phrases naturally:

- kaos custom,
- merchandise creator,
- blank apparel,
- kaos heavyweight.

---

# 79. Brand Claim Hierarchy

Claims are divided into:

## Level 1 — Factual

Example:

> Made to order.

## Level 2 — Demonstrable

Example:

> Every printed order passes QC.

## Level 3 — Subjective

Example:

> Designed for a cleaner everyday fit.

## Level 4 — Superlative

Example:

> The best tee in Indonesia.

Level 4 should generally be avoided unless independently substantiated and strategically necessary.

---

# 80. Sustainability Claims

Never use:

```text
eco-friendly
sustainable
green
ethical
```

without clear evidence and defined scope.

Preferred:

> Packaging uses X.

Specific > broad claim.

---

# 81. IP Copy

Do not call:

> original TeeStock design

unless IP classification confirms internal ownership.

Use:

```text
TeeStock Selects
Artwork by ...
Licensed artwork
TeeStock Original
Collaboration
```

according to actual rights.

---

# 82. Creator Attribution

Recommended:

> Artwork by [Creator].

For collaborations:

> TeeStock × [Creator].

Attribution must follow contract.

---

# 83. Product Naming Taxonomy

Recommended naming structure:

```text
[Brand / Line]
[Product Type]
[Design / Collection]
[Variant]
```

Example:

```text
Do Your Best
Essential Tee
Start Anyway
Black
```

Avoid unnecessarily poetic product names that hide product type.

---

# 84. Collection Naming

Collection names may be more expressive.

Recommended:

```text
Chapter 001 — Start Anyway
The Night Shift
Version 0.1
```

But collection naming should support brand worldview.

---

# 85. Internal vs External Language

Internal:

```text
SKU
COGS
SLA
POD
CM2
```

External consumer:

translate when necessary.

Example:

Internal:

> Production SLA: H+3.

External:

> Pesanan diproses maksimal 3 hari kerja.

---

# 86. Technical Language Rule

Use technical terminology when it helps decision-making.

Example:

> 24s cotton

should be followed by:

> feel lebih padat dibanding tee yang lebih tipis.

## Homepage

Low density.

## Product Page

Medium.

## Service Page

Medium-high.

High.

Very low.

Context determines density.

```text
WHAT CAN I DO?
WHY TRUST IT?
WHERE DO I GO?
```

# 91. Navigation Labels

Use simple labels.

Preferred:

```text
Shop
Custom
Business
Merch
Originals
```

Avoid clever:

```text
The Lab

The Works
```

if clarity suffers.

---

# 92. Microcopy Principle

Good microcopy should reduce uncertainty.

Before click:

> what happens?

After click:

> what happened?

Next:

> what should I do?

---

# 93. Conversion Copy Principle

Conversion does not require aggressive copy.

Conversion improves when uncertainty decreases.

Focus on:

```text
PRICE
FIT
DELIVERY
RETURNS
QUALITY
TRUST
```

---

# 94. Copy Testing

Copy can be tested for:

- comprehension,
- conversion,
- completion rate,
- support reduction.

But tests cannot violate core brand standards.

---

# 95. AI-Generated Copy Rule

AI may assist writing.

AI-generated copy must still obey:

- voice,
- claims policy,
- IP classification,
- pricing truth,
- product data.

AI cannot invent:

- specs,
- stock,
- certifications,
- guarantees,
- ownership.

---

# 96. Source-of-Truth Rule

Product copy must derive factual information from canonical product data.

Example:

```text
MATERIAL
FIT
GSM
CARE
PRODUCTION
STOCK
```

Copy layer cannot redefine product facts.

---

# 97. Dynamic Copy

Personalization may change:

- recommendation,

# 103. Copy Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

## Corporate Fog

> Kami senantiasa berkomitmen menghadirkan solusi terbaik.

Meaningless without proof.

## Hype Spam

> WAJIB PUNYA!!! 🔥🔥🔥

## Feature Dump

Listing specs without benefits.

## Fake Urgency

Permanent countdown.

## Fashion Gatekeeping

Making audience feel inferior.

## Startup Jargon

> An integrated omnichannel apparel enablement solution.

unless internal/investor context genuinely requires it.
