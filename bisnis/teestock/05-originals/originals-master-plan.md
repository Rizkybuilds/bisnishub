# TeeStock Originals Master Plan v1.0

> **Canonical Originals & Consumer IP Strategy**  
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan bagaimana TeeStock menciptakan, menguji, mengembangkan, mengoperasikan, mengukur, mempromosikan, menskalakan, dan menghentikan consumer intellectual property melalui Collections dan Independent Labels.

---

# 1. Purpose

TeeStock Originals menjawab:

> **Apa yang TeeStock ciptakan dan miliki sendiri sebagai consumer intellectual property?**

Canonical principle:

> **We create it.**

Jika Selects adalah:

```text id="org01"
We curate it.
```

maka Originals adalah:

```text id="org02"
We create it.
```

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Originals adalah consumer IP dan brand-creation division yang menciptakan, menguji, memiliki, dan mengembangkan apparel concepts, collections, characters, narratives, visual systems, dan independent consumer labels di bawah ecosystem TeeStock.**

---

# 3. Originals Is Not a Commerce Line

Critical distinction:

```text id="org03"
ORIGINALS
creates and owns consumer IP.

COMMERCE
sells products.
```

Originals products dapat dijual melalui TeeStock Commerce.

Tetapi Originals secara struktural tetap merupakan:

```text id="org04"
IP / BRAND CREATION DIVISION
```

---

# 4. Strategic Role

Originals memiliki lima fungsi utama:

```text id="org05"
IP OWNERSHIP
+
BRAND EQUITY
+
HIGHER LONG-TERM MARGIN POTENTIAL
+
CULTURAL DIFFERENTIATION
+
PORTFOLIO OPTIONALITY
```

---

# 5. Why Originals Matters

Commerce, Services, dan Programs dapat menghasilkan:

- revenue,
- transaction,
- operational scale.

Originals dapat menambah:

```text id="org06"
OWNED DEMAND
+
OWNED STORY
+
OWNED AUDIENCE RELATIONSHIP
+
OWNED IP VALUE
```

---

# 6. Economic Role

Canonical:

```text id="org07"
SELECTS
monetizes curation.

SERVICES
monetizes capability.

ORIGINALS
monetizes owned IP and brand equity.
```

---

# 7. Long-Term Strategic Thesis

Jika TeeStock hanya menjual produk dan capability:

nilai utama tetap berada pada:

- execution,
- margin,
- distribution.

Jika TeeStock juga memiliki consumer IP:

nilai tambahan dapat berada pada:

```text id="org08"
BRAND
COMMUNITY
CULTURAL RELEVANCE
REPEAT DEMAND
LICENSING
PORTFOLIO EQUITY
```

---

# 8. Originals Architecture

Canonical structure:

```text id="org09"
TEEStock ORIGINALS
│
├── COLLECTIONS
│
└── INDEPENDENT LABELS
```

---

# 9. Collection

## Definition

> **Collection adalah controlled consumer-IP experiment atau product grouping yang memiliki creative idea, narrative, dan visual cohesion tetapi belum memiliki cukup evidence untuk menjadi Independent Label.**

---

# 10. Independent Label

## Definition

> **Independent Label adalah consumer brand yang telah membuktikan bahwa ia memiliki belief, audience, identity, product universe, demand, dan strategic potential yang cukup untuk beroperasi sebagai brand identity yang lebih mandiri.**

---

# 11. Collection ≠ Label

Canonical:

```text id="org10"
COLLECTION
tests an idea.

LABEL
builds a world.
```

A Collection can succeed commercially without becoming Label.

# 13. Insight

Insight is not yet a product idea.

It is an observation about:

- behavior,
- identity,
- tension,
- aspiration,
- culture,
- audience.

Example:

> Banyak pekerja kreatif membangun side project setelah jam kerja karena pekerjaan utama tidak cukup menyalurkan ambisi personal mereka.

This can inspire:

```text id="org12"
After Hours Club
```

But the insight and brand are not the same.

---

# 15. Selects as Signal Source

- recurring themes,

> **Originals should never become a machine that merely copies best-selling Selects graphics.**


# 17. Belief

Independent consumer IP should usually express something meaningful.

Example conceptual:

```text id="org15"
DO YOUR BEST
Healthy ambition over perfection.

WORK IN PROGRESS
Identity is always evolving.

AFTER HOURS CLUB
Build what matters after the day job ends.
```

These are conceptual examples.

---

# 18. Belief vs Slogan

A slogan is wording.

A belief is:

> the underlying worldview that can generate many products, stories, and behaviors.

Strong label potential requires belief deeper than one phrase.

---

# 19. Audience Hypothesis

Every Originals concept should answer:

```text id="org16"

WHY WOULD THEY CARE?

WHAT IDENTITY DOES IT EXPRESS?
```

# 182. Originals Failure Modes

## Brand Before Demand

Builds infrastructure around an assumption.

## Collection = Label

Creates too many weak brands.

## Trend Copying

No owned meaning.

## Too Much Inventory

Capital trapped in unvalidated idea.

## Full Brand System Too Early

Creative overinvestment before market proof.

## One Viral Product = Brand

False promotion signal.

## Separate Accounts Everywhere

Fragmented attention.

## No Kill Discipline

Portfolio becomes graveyard of half-active brands.
