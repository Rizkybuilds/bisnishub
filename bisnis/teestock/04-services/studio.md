# 1. Purpose

TeeStock Studio menjawab:

> **Bagaimana TeeStock mengubah ide, requirement, atau brand direction menjadi creative asset yang benar-benar siap digunakan di apparel dan merchandise?**

Canonical principle:

> **Creative work that is designed to become real products.**

Studio harus terasa:

```text id="std01"
CREATIVE
+
PRACTICAL
+
PRODUCTION-AWARE
```

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Studio adalah apparel-focused creative and product-development capability yang membantu TeeStock maupun customer mengembangkan graphic, apparel concept, merchandise system, packaging, mockup, dan production-ready creative assets.**

---

# 3. Strategic Role

Studio memiliki empat fungsi utama:

```text id="std02"
CREATIVE CAPABILITY
+
PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT
+
INTERNAL SUPPORT
+
EXTERNAL SERVICE REVENUE
```

---

# 4. Internal vs External Mode

Studio bekerja dalam dua mode.

## Internal Studio

Mendukung:

```text id="std03"
Selects
Originals
Commerce
Business
Merch
Marketing
```

## External Studio

Menjual creative service kepada customer yang relevan.

---

# 5. Why Studio Exists

Tanpa Studio, creative work berisiko:

- tidak konsisten,
- tidak production-aware,
- terlalu bergantung freelancer,
- sulit diwariskan,
- sulit distandardisasi.

Studio menjadi bridge:

```text id="std04"
IDEA
↓
CREATIVE
↓
PRODUCT
↓
PRODUCTION
```

---

# 6. Positioning

TeeStock Studio bukan:

- general design agency,
- social media agency,
- video production house,
- full branding consultancy untuk semua industri.

Positioning:

> **Creative studio for apparel, merchandise, and product expression.**

# 30. Concept Stage

Studio should present a limited number of strong concepts.

Avoid:

> 20 random options so customer chooses.

More options do not equal better creative process.

---

# 31. Concept Approval

Once direction approved:

```text id="std19"
CONCEPT LOCK
```

Major change later becomes Change Request.

---

# 32. Revision

Revision means:

> improving an approved direction.

Not:

> generating a new direction from scratch repeatedly.

---

# 33. Revision Policy

Each service should define:

```text id="std20"

```

Additional revision can be charged.

# 34. Revision Classification


# 35. Scope Creep

- mockup becomes campaign shoot.

# 43. Default External Work

For paid custom creative work, ownership/license should be defined in quote/agreement.

# 44. Internal Work


# 46. Source Files

- production exports only,
- editable source files,

# 47. Fonts and Assets

# 48. Stock Assets

# 51. Creative Originality

Studio should avoid:

- imitation too close to reference,
- trademark mimicry,
- recognizable artist copying.

# 121. Studio Failure Modes

## No Brief

Revision chaos.

## Unlimited Concepts

Creative waste.

## Unlimited Revisions

Margin collapse.

## No Production Review

Beautiful but unusable work.

## Rights Unclear

Legal risk.
