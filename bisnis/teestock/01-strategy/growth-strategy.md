# TeeStock Growth Strategy v1.0

> [!abstract] **Canonical Growth Strategy Document  **
> Dokumen ini mendefinisikan bagaimana TeeStock berkembang secara bertahap dari commerce operation sederhana menjadi apparel commerce, services, brand, dan infrastructure ecosystem tanpa kehilangan fokus, cash discipline, dan operational clarity.

# 1. Purpose

Growth Strategy menjawab:

- apa yang harus dibangun terlebih dahulu,
- capability mana yang harus menunggu,
- kapan sebuah business line layak diaktifkan,
- bagaimana TeeStock berpindah dari manual ke automation,
- bagaimana TeeStock memperluas revenue tanpa menciptakan operational chaos,
- dan apa exit criteria sebelum berpindah ke fase berikutnya.

Core principle:

> **Growth harus mengikuti proof, bukan excitement.**

---

# 2. Growth Philosophy

TeeStock tidak menggunakan pola:

```text
IDEA
↓
BUILD EVERYTHING
↓
LAUNCH EVERYTHING
↓
HOPE
```

Canonical approach:

```text
FOCUS
↓
VALIDATE
↓
STANDARDIZE
↓
SYSTEMIZE
↓
AUTOMATE
↓
EXPAND
↓
PLATFORMIZE
```

Setiap tahap baru harus menyelesaikan bottleneck nyata.

---

# 3. Core Growth Principle

TeeStock berkembang melalui tiga jenis leverage:

```text
DEMAND LEVERAGE
+
OPERATIONAL LEVERAGE
+
IP LEVERAGE
```

## Demand Leverage

Lebih banyak customer melalui:

- brand,
- creators,
- channels,
- reseller,
- affiliates,
- repeat customers.

## Operational Leverage

Lebih banyak output tanpa peningkatan workload linear melalui:

- standardization,
- automation,
- shared infrastructure,
- MGBOS.

## IP Leverage

Membangun asset yang dapat menghasilkan demand berulang:

- Originals,
- Independent Labels,
- original design systems,
- brand equity.

---

# 4. Growth Guardrail

Pertumbuhan dianggap sehat jika:

```text
Revenue ↑
Contribution Margin healthy
Operational Error controlled
Customer Experience stable
Founder Load manageable
Cash Flow controlled
```

Jika hanya revenue yang naik tetapi:

- workload meledak,
- margin turun,
- complaint naik,
- inventory menumpuk,

maka growth tersebut dianggap:

> **low-quality growth.**

# 6. Phase 0 — Foundation

## Objective

Membangun fondasi sebelum mengejar volume.

Focus:

```text
Business Definition
Brand Architecture
Product Standard
Supplier Setup
Production Standard
Pricing Logic
Basic Commerce
Basic Finance
Basic Data
```

---

## Required Capabilities

Minimal harus tersedia:

- canonical documentation,
- product taxonomy,
- SKU convention,
- core supplier,
- basic production workflow,
- basic inventory tracking,
- order tracking,
- payment flow,
- customer support flow,
- simple finance tracking.

---

## Exit Criteria

Phase 0 selesai jika:

```text
Offering jelas
Product dapat diproduksi
Order dapat diterima
Order dapat dipenuhi
Revenue dan cost dapat dicatat
```

Belum perlu automation kompleks.

---

# 7. Phase 1 — Commerce Validation

## Strategic Objective

Membuktikan bahwa orang bersedia membeli apparel TeeStock secara nyata.

Primary focus:

```text
TeeStock Selects
+
TeeStock Essentials
```

Originals masih boleh hadir sebagai experiment kecil, bukan sebagai portfolio besar.

---

# 8. Phase 1 Questions

TeeStock harus menjawab:

- produk mana yang benar-benar dibeli?
- niche mana yang menghasilkan conversion?
- garment mana yang paling disukai?
- warna/size mana yang bergerak?
- harga mana yang diterima?
- channel mana yang menghasilkan customer?
- apakah customer puas?
- apakah mereka kembali membeli?

---

# 9. Phase 1 Product Strategy

Catalog harus sempit tetapi cukup informatif.

Prinsip:

```text
FEWER PRODUCTS
BETTER LEARNING
```

Bukan:

```text
HUNDREDS OF SKUS
NO SIGNAL
```

Gunakan limited:

- base garments,
- colors,
- sizes,
- design themes.

---

# 10. Phase 1 Inventory Strategy

Priority:

```text
BASE GARMENT INVENTORY
+
ON-DEMAND DECORATION
```

Hindari finished-goods inventory besar.

Tujuan:

> meminimalkan modal mati sambil mempelajari demand.

---

# 11. Phase 1 Exit Criteria

Commerce dapat dianggap validated jika terdapat kombinasi:

```text
REAL ORDERS
+
POSITIVE CONTRIBUTION
+
REPEATABLE FULFILLMENT
+
CUSTOMER SATISFACTION
```

Recommended operational evidence:

- order nyata konsisten,
- fulfillment tanpa chaos,
- return/defect terkendali,
- margin tidak negatif,
- customer feedback mulai terbentuk.

Tidak perlu menunggu revenue besar.

---

# 12. Phase 2 — Service Validation

Setelah basic Commerce stabil, TeeStock dapat mengaktifkan Services prioritas.

Canonical order:

```text
Custom
↓
Business
```

Bukan langsung semua enam Services.

---

# 13. Why Custom First

Custom dekat dengan existing capability.

TeeStock sudah memiliki:

```text
Garment
+
Decoration
+
Production
+
Fulfillment
```

Custom hanya menambahkan:

```text
Customer-specific configuration
```

Ini merupakan adjacency yang natural.

---

# 14. Custom Validation Goals

Buktikan:

- customer mau membayar,
- requirement dapat distandardisasi,
- quote dapat dibuat konsisten,
- revision burden terkendali,
- production dapat dipenuhi tanpa mengganggu Commerce.

---

# 15. Custom Exit Criteria

Custom dianggap sehat jika:

```text
Lead
→ Quote
→ Order
→ Production
→ Delivery
```

dapat dilakukan secara repeatable.

Measure:

- quote-to-order conversion,
- average contribution,
- revision count,
- turnaround time,
- founder time/order.

---

# 16. Business Activation

TeeStock Business diaktifkan setelah Custom workflow cukup matang.

Reason:

B2B membawa tambahan complexity:

- larger quantity,
- quotation,
- invoicing,
- approval,
- specification,
- payment terms.

Namun sebagian besar capability dasarnya sama.

---

# 17. Business Validation

Buktikan:

- beberapa order organisasi nyata,
- pricing mampu menangani quantity tier,
- procurement stabil,
- delivery reliable,
- repeat order memungkinkan.

---

# 18. Phase 2 Exit Criteria

Phase 2 selesai jika TeeStock dapat menjalankan:

```text
B2C COMMERCE
+
CUSTOM
+
BASIC B2B
```

tanpa founder kehilangan kendali operasional.

---

# 19. Phase 3 — Distribution Network

Setelah product dan fulfillment terbukti, TeeStock mulai memperluas distribution.

Primary Programs:

```text
Creator Program
Reseller Program
Affiliate Program
```

Tidak semuanya harus dimulai bersamaan.

---

# 20. Distribution Principle

Growth tidak harus selalu datang dari:

```text
MORE ADS
```

TeeStock harus mengembangkan:

```text
OWNED DEMAND
+
PARTNER DEMAND
+
NETWORK DEMAND
```

---

# 21. Reseller Activation

Mulai sederhana.

Early model:

```text
Manual onboarding
Shared catalog
Special price
Manual order
TeeStock fulfillment
```

Jangan langsung membangun portal reseller kompleks.

---

# 22. Reseller Validation

Measure:

- active reseller count,
- order frequency,
- contribution after reseller discount,
- operational burden,
- return/error rate.

Portal baru dibangun jika manual workflow menjadi bottleneck.

---

# 23. Affiliate Activation

Affiliate dapat menjadi lighter distribution model.

Suitable when:

- catalog stabil,
- attribution tersedia,
- commission economics jelas.

Affiliate sebaiknya menggunakan:

```text
Performance-based payout
```

bukan fixed expense besar.

---

# 24. Creator Program Activation

Creator Program dapat dimulai sebagai collaboration program kecil.

Early model:

```text
Select creator
↓
Select artwork
↓
Co-drop
↓
Measure sales
↓
Pay royalty
```

Tujuan pertama:

> membuktikan creator audience dapat menghasilkan transaksi.

---

# 25. Phase 3 Exit Criteria

Distribution Network dianggap terbukti jika:

```text
NON-FOUNDER DISTRIBUTION
```

mulai menghasilkan persentase meaningful dari demand.

TeeStock tidak lagi hanya bergantung pada:

- personal network,
- founder posting,
- paid ads.

---

# 26. Phase 4 — Creator & Merch Engine

Setelah creator collaboration terbukti, TeeStock dapat mengembangkan:

```text
TeeStock Merch
```

dari collaboration menjadi service line.

---

# 27. Merch Growth Stages

## Stage A — Co-Drop

Creator masuk ke TeeStock storefront.

## Stage B — Creator Collection

Creator memiliki dedicated collection/page.

## Stage C — Managed Merch

TeeStock mengelola:

- product,
- production,
- fulfillment.

## Stage D — Creator Store

Creator memperoleh storefront lebih independen.

## Stage E — Custom Domain

Hanya untuk creator dengan demand cukup besar.

---

# 28. Merch Platformization Rule

Jangan bangun:

- multi-tenant dashboard,
- automated payouts,
- custom domain engine

sebelum manual Merch operation memiliki enough volume.

Canonical rule:

```text
MANUAL DEMAND
↓
REPEAT DEMAND
↓
SYSTEM
↓
PLATFORM
```

---

# 29. Phase 4 Exit Criteria

Creator/Merch dianggap validated jika:

- creator aktif bertambah,
- creator melakukan repeat launch,
- merch menghasilkan contribution sehat,
- payout dapat dikelola,
- fulfillment tetap reliable.

---

# 30. Phase 5 — Originals Incubation

Originals sebaiknya tidak menunggu seluruh bisnis besar.

Eksperimen dapat dimulai lebih awal.

Tetapi **serious brand incubation** dilakukan setelah TeeStock sudah memiliki:

```text
Market Data
+
Product Capability
+
Commerce Capability
+
Distribution
```

# 32. Collection Before Label

Default assumption:

> setiap konsep baru adalah Collection.

Bukan Independent Label.

Label hanya dibuat jika collection menunjukkan:

- repeated demand,
- identity strength,
- creative extensibility,
- community potential,
- economic potential.

---

# 33. Independent Label Promotion Criteria

Sebuah concept dapat dipromosikan menjadi Label jika memenuhi sebagian besar:

```text
Distinct worldview
Distinct audience
Distinct visual system
Repeatable product ideas
Multiple collection potential
Organic recognition
Repeat customer interest
Positive economics
```

---

# 34. Originals Portfolio Strategy

TeeStock tidak boleh memiliki terlalu banyak Labels.

Portfolio rule:

```text
FEW STRONG LABELS
>
MANY WEAK LABELS
```

TeeStock Originals bertindak sebagai incubator dan portfolio manager.

---

# 35. Phase 5 Exit Criteria

Originals ecosystem mulai matang jika minimal satu concept menunjukkan:

```text
REPEATABLE IDENTITY-DRIVEN DEMAND
```

bukan hanya satu design viral.

---

# 36. Phase 6 — Supply & Fulfillment Scale

Supply dan Fulfill bukan prioritas awal karena:

- working capital,
- operations,
- logistics,
- inventory complexity

dapat meningkat cepat.

Mereka diaktifkan lebih serius ketika existing ecosystem menciptakan volume.

---

# 37. Supply Scaling Logic

Supply menjadi menarik ketika:

```text
Internal Procurement Volume
+
External B2B Demand
```

cukup untuk memberikan procurement leverage.

Supply tidak boleh berkembang hanya untuk mengejar omzet besar dengan margin tipis.

---

# 38. Fulfill Scaling Logic

Fulfill berkembang ketika:

- creator volume naik,
- reseller volume naik,
- Independent Labels bertambah,
- external brands meminta fulfillment.

---

# 39. Fulfill Exit Criteria

Fulfill dianggap scalable jika dapat mengukur:

```text
Cost per Order
Pick Accuracy
Pack Accuracy
Shipment SLA
Return Rate
Error Rate
```

tanpa bergantung pada memory founder.

---

# 40. Phase 7 — Platformization

Platformization adalah fase akhir, bukan starting point.

Potential platform capabilities:

```text
Creator Dashboard
Partner Portal
Reseller Portal
Multi-tenant Storefront
Automated Payouts
Self-service Product Builder
Fulfillment Dashboard
API
```

---

# 41. Platformization Criteria

Sebuah workflow hanya boleh dip-platform-kan jika:

```text
HIGH FREQUENCY
+
REPEATABLE
+
STANDARDIZED
+
EXPENSIVE TO DO MANUALLY
```

Jika tidak:

> manual process may remain superior.

---

# 42. AI Operations Stage

AI adoption mengikuti maturity.

## Stage 1 — Assist

AI membantu:

- copy,
- research,
- analysis,
- support draft.

## Stage 2 — Recommend

AI memberikan:

- reorder suggestions,
- pricing alerts,
- content recommendations.

## Stage 3 — Execute

AI menjalankan low-risk workflow.

## Stage 4 — Orchestrate

AI mengkoordinasikan multiple workflows.

---

# 43. AI Guardrail

High-impact decisions tetap membutuhkan explicit policy.

Contoh:

```text
Large spend
Pricing override
Refund above threshold
IP publication
Vendor replacement
Large production commitment
```

# 44. Growth by Bottleneck

TeeStock tidak berkembang berdasarkan calendar saja.

Growth harus mengikuti bottleneck.

```text
If demand low
→ improve product/distribution

If capacity low
→ improve operations

If conversion low
→ improve offer/storefront

If margin low
→ improve economics

If founder overloaded
→ standardize/automate

If customer retention low
→ improve experience/product
```


# 50. Capital Allocation Principle

Modal diprioritaskan pada:

```text
Validated Demand
Operational Bottlenecks
High-velocity Inventory
High-leverage Systems
Strong IP
```

Hindari modal besar untuk:

- speculative inventory,
- vanity infrastructure,
- unvalidated software.

---

# 51. Inventory Growth Rule

Inventory hanya boleh naik jika data menunjukkan:

```text
Demand Frequency
+
Turnover
+
Supply Lead Time
```

membenarkan.

Recommended mindset:

```text
Buy information cheaply first.
Buy inventory later.
```

---

# 52. Channel Expansion Rule

Jangan buka semua channel sekaligus.

New channel harus menjawab:

- audience baru?
- lower CAC?
- higher convenience?
- strategic visibility?
- better conversion?

Jika tidak, channel hanya menambah operational surface.

---

# 53. Geographic Expansion Rule

TeeStock tidak membutuhkan ekspansi geografis fisik sebelum:

- logistics menunjukkan bottleneck,
- demand cluster jelas,
- shipping economics membenarkan.

Digital reach dapat nasional lebih dulu.

Physical infrastructure mengikuti data.

---

# 54. Product Expansion Rule

Product category baru harus memiliki adjacency.

Contoh baik:

```text
T-Shirt
→ Hoodie
→ Tote
→ Merch Accessories
```

Contoh lebih jauh:

```text
T-Shirt
→ unrelated electronics
```

harus dianggap di luar strategic scope.

---

# 55. Service Expansion Rule

Service baru hanya ditambahkan jika menggunakan capability existing atau memperkuat ecosystem.

Contoh:

```text
Custom
→ Business
→ Merch
```

natural adjacency.

---

# 56. Growth Experiments
