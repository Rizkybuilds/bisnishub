Dokumen ini mendefinisikan bagaimana TeeStock menciptakan, mengevaluasi, mengadopsi, mendaftarkan, menggunakan, melisensikan, memonitor, memperpanjang, mempertahankan, dan menghentikan penggunaan nama merek, logo, label, collection marks, dan commercial identifiers.

---

# 1. Purpose

Trademark Framework menjawab:

> **Bagaimana TeeStock memastikan sebuah nama atau identitas brand cukup aman untuk dibangun sebelum perusahaan menginvestasikan produk, konten, packaging, distribusi, creator network, marketing budget, dan reputasi di atasnya?**

Canonical principle:

> **Validate the name before compounding value into it.**

---

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Trademark Framework adalah governed brand-protection system yang menghubungkan naming, clearance, filing, registration, classification, ownership, permitted use, licensing, monitoring, renewal, conflict handling, and brand-portfolio decisions ke dalam satu lifecycle yang dapat dilacak melalui MGBOS.**

---

# 3. Trademark Is a Business Asset

Trademark bukan sekadar:

```text
LOGO
```

```text
CUSTOMER RECOGNITION
+
REPUTATION
+
DISTRIBUTION ADVANTAGE
+
LICENSING ASSET
+
LONG-TERM BRAND EQUITY
```

---

# 4. Indonesian Registration Principle

Hak atas merek di Indonesia diperoleh setelah merek terdaftar dalam sistem pendaftaran yang bersifat konstitutif / first-to-file.

Operational implication:

```text
USING A NAME
≠
OWNING A REGISTERED TRADEMARK RIGHT
```

---

# 5. Filing Priority Matters

Canonical:

> **A strategically important brand should not remain indefinitely in “we've been using it for years” mode without a registration strategy.**

TRADEMARK OWNERSHIP SHOULD BE DELIBERATE.

TRADEMARK LICENSES SHOULD HAVE QUALITY AND USAGE CONTROLS.

AI MAY SEARCH, CLUSTER, AND FLAG. AUTHORIZED HUMANS/LEGAL PROFESSIONALS CLEAR, FILE, TRANSFER, AND ENFORCE.
