# 1. Purpose

IP Policy menjawab:

> **Atas dasar hak apa TeeStock boleh membuat, mencetak, menjual, mempromosikan, memodifikasi, menyimpan, mendistribusikan, atau melisensikan sebuah karya, desain, brand asset, atau intellectual property?**

Canonical principle:

> **No documented rights basis. No commercial use.**

# 2. Canonical Definition

> **TeeStock Intellectual Property Policy adalah governance framework yang memastikan setiap intellectual property asset memiliki identifiable source, ownership status, permitted uses, evidence, restrictions, effective period, responsible party, dan rights-clearance status sebelum digunakan untuk production, commerce, marketing, collaboration, licensing, atau other commercial activities.**

# 5. Indonesian Copyright Baseline

Under Indonesia's current Copyright Act, copyright protection generally arises automatically when a qualifying creation is expressed in tangible form; registration/recordation is therefore not the event that creates copyright itself.

Operational implication:

```text
NO REGISTRATION
≠
NO RIGHTS
```

TeeStock must not assume an internet image is free merely because no registration notice exists.

---

# 6. Trademark Baseline

Indonesia's principal trademark framework remains Law No. 20 of 2016 on Trademarks and Geographical Indications, which the current legal database notes has been affected by Cipta Kerja legislation.

Operational implication:

```text
BRAND NAME AVAILABLE ONLINE
≠
TRADEMARK CLEAR
```

---

# 7. Industrial Design Baseline

Product appearance may also implicate Indonesia's industrial-design regime under Law No. 31 of 2000, which treats registered industrial designs as a distinct form of IP right.

NO DOCUMENTED RIGHTS BASIS. NO COMMERCIAL USE.

PAYMENT IS NOT AUTOMATIC IP ASSIGNMENT.

ATTRIBUTION IS NOT PERMISSION.

OWNERSHIP IS NOT LICENSE.

CURATION DOES NOT REPLACE CLEARANCE.

CUSTOMER SUBMISSION DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY PROVE OWNERSHIP.

TRADEMARK AVAILABILITY IS NOT DOMAIN AVAILABILITY.

AI GENERATION IS NOT A RIGHTS-CLEARANCE SHORTCUT.

AI MAY ASSIST IP OPERATIONS. HUMANS AND GOVERNED LEGAL PROCESS DECIDE MATERIAL RIGHTS ISSUES.

PRODUCTION, COMMERCE, AND MARKETING SHOULD RESPECT THE SAME RIGHTS STATE.

IP SHOULD BE MANAGED AS STRUCTURED BUSINESS DATA, NOT A FOLDER OF CONTRACTS.

TEEStock OWNS THE RESPONSIBILITY TO OPERATE A CONTROLLED RIGHTS PROCESS.
