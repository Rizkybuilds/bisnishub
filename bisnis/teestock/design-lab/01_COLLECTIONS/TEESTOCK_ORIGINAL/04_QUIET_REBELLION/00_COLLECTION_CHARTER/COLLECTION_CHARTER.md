## 3. BAHASA VISUAL & ARAH SENI
- **Visual Direction:** Underground editorial, bold brutalist typography, stark symbolic graphics, punk-influenced high-contrast monochrome design.
- **Color Palette:** #121212 (Ink Black), #FFFFFF (Pure White), #E65100 (Hazard Orange accent), #757575 (Ashen Gray)
- **Typography:** Heavy compressed sans-serif (Druk / Impact / Knockout) dipadukan dengan archival typewriter (Special Elite).

---

## 4. STANDAR TEKNIS PRODUKSI DTF
- Native 300 PPI canvas pada skala fisik cetak.
- Garis isolasi minimum >= 0.5 mm.
- Knockout black pada kaos gelap agar bahan tetap lentur dan adem.
