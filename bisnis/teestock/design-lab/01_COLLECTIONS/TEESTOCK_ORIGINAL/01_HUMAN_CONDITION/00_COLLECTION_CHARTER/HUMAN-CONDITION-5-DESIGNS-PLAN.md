### Artikel 01: `TS-OR-HC001` — "Stupidity (The Sleep of Reason)"
- **Pesan Inti:** Kebodohan manusia bukan karena ketiadaan informasi, melainkan akibat penolakan untuk berpikir kritis secara mandiri.
- **Warna Kaos Dasar:** Heavyweight Black / Vintage Charcoal.

#### Pemisahan Komponen:
1. **Primary Illustration (AI Generated):**  
   Ilustrasi etsa tembaga murni: tengkorak manusia tampak samping yang terbelah rapi, memperlihatkan susunan mekanisme jam mekanik kuno yang macet.
2. **Typography (Manual di Inkscape):**  
   - Headline: `"STUPIDITY"` (Font: *Cinzel Decorative*, All Caps, tracking renggang).  
   - Sub-headline: `"THE SLEEP OF REASON PRODUCES MONSTERS"` (Font: *Space Mono*, All Caps).  
   - Editorial footnote: `"VOL. 01 — TEEStock ARCHIVAL SERIES — REF. HC-001"` (Font: *Archivo Narrow*).
3. **Supporting Elements (Manual Vektor):**  
   Kotak pembatas etsa tipis (*hairline border*), koordinat grid `[LAT. 00° // LONG. 00°]`.
4. **Texture (Krita/Photopea):**  
   Efek *distressed ink bleed* halus dan *Knockout Black* pada seluruh bayangan hitam pekat.

#### Master Prompt Specification:
- **Generation Prompt (Ideogram / ChatGPT):**
  > `A detailed 19th-century vintage woodcut etching of a human skull seen in profile, intricately fused with an antique mechanical clockwork mechanism inside the brain cavity. Albrecht Dürer engraving style, fine cross-hatching linework, stippling shading, high-contrast black ink illustration isolated on pure white background, zero text, flat 2D vector-like aesthetic for apparel print, no digital 3D gradients, no realistic skin tones.`
- **Refinement Prompt:**
  > `Preserve the exact profile composition and skull anatomy. Enhance the linework sharpness of the interior clockwork gears, make the shading purely composed of clean etched lines, remove any blurry grey halos.`
- **Production Instruction:**
  > `Photopea: Invert/adjust levels to aged bone (#F4EFE6) for black garment print. Apply neon defringe test. Combine with vector typography layers from Inkscape.`

### Artikel 02: `TS-OR-HC002` — "The Great Distortion"
- **Pesan Inti:** Persepsi manusia tentang realitas telah terdistorsi secara permanen oleh ilusi media sosial.
- **Warna Kaos Dasar:** Washed Black / Bone White.

#### Pemisahan Komponen:
1. **Primary Illustration (AI Generated):**  
   Patung marmer klasik Romawi yang kepalanya terbelah horizontal oleh lempeng prisma kaca surealis.
2. **Typography (Manual):**  
   - Headline: `"DISTORTION"` (Font: *Bodoni Moda Bold*, dengan distorsi tracking dinamis).  
   - Micro-text: `"PERCEPTION IS NOT REALITY // FILTERED THROUGH GLASS"`.
3. **Supporting Elements:**  
   Garis ukur teknis optik, lingkaran target fokus lensa kamera.
4. **Texture:**  
   Xerox grain fotokopi kasar pada pinggiran patung.

#### Master Prompt Specification:
- **Generation Prompt:**
  > `An archival copperplate etching of an ancient Greek marble statue bust, cleanly bisected horizontally through the forehead with surreal refraction effect. Classical etching linework, detailed cross-hatching, ink on paper texture, pure white background, monochrome black ink, apparel print design, no modern clothing, no colorful artifacts, flat graphic presentation.`

- **Warna Kaos Dasar:** Heavyweight Bone White / Sand.

#### Pemisahan Komponen:
1. **Primary Illustration (AI Generated):**  
   Dua tangan klasik bergaya sketsa Renaisans yang saling melepaskan lingkaran cahaya hitam pekat di tengahnya.
2. **Typography (Manual):**

   - Body quote: `"TO LOSE ONESELF IS TO BECOME EVERYTHING."`
3. **Supporting Elements:**  
   Diagram busur derajat, lingkaran geometri sakral tipis.
4. **Texture:**  
   Halftone dots 45 derajat halus pada bayangan tangan.

#### Master Prompt Specification:
- **Generation Prompt:**

Leonardo da Vinci sketchbook etching style, sepia-black ink on plain white background, fine detailed linework, no background clutter, flat graphic composition for chest t-shirt print.`

### Artikel 04: `TS-OR-HC004` — "Manufactured Consent"
- **Pesan Inti:** Opini massa yang tampak sukarela sebenarnya telah diprogram oleh algoritma dan struktur kekuasaan.
- **Warna Kaos Dasar:** Black / Dark Slate Navy.

#### Pemisahan Komponen:
1. **Primary Illustration (AI Generated):**  
   Mata manusia etsa kuno berukuran besar yang dikelilingi oleh jaring-jaring kabel atau benang manipulasi wayang.
2. **Typography (Manual):**  
   - Headline: `"MANUFACTURED CONSENT"` (Font: *Archivo Narrow Bold*, huruf kapital tegas).  
   - Editorial badge: `"SYS. ARCHIVE 2026 // COGNITIVE BIAS DIVISION"`.
3. **Supporting Elements:**  
   Tabel parameter palsu (*data specs box*), barcode vertikal di sisi kanan bawah.
4. **Texture:**  
   Stippled ink texture, sedikit distressing pada garis luar kotak.

#### Master Prompt Specification:
- **Generation Prompt:**
  > `Detailed vintage botanical and medical etching of an all-seeing human eye intricately connected to delicate puppet strings and wires. 18th-century scientific diagram style, high-contrast monochrome linework, pure white background, sharp vector-like boundaries, apparel graphic print ready, no photorealism, no 3D rendering.`

### Artikel 05: `TS-OR-HC005` — "Digital Lobotomy"
- **Pesan Inti:** Rentang perhatian manusia yang dipenggal secara sukarela demi konsumsi konten instan tanpa henti.
- **Warna Kaos Dasar:** Heavyweight Black.

#### Pemisahan Komponen:
1. **Primary Illustration (AI Generated):**  
   Kepala figur klasik dari belakang dengan jendela persegi terbuka di bagian tengkorak, seekor gagak hinggap di tepiannya.
2. **Typography (Manual):**  
   - Headline: `"DIGITAL LOBOTOMY"` (Font: *Playfair Display Black*).  
   - Ironical tagline: `"ENTERTAINED TO EXTINCTION"`.
3. **Supporting Elements:**  
   Stempel oval *"TEEStock ORIGINAL APPROVED"*, nomor seri arsip.
4. **Texture:**  
   *Knockout black* intensif, *newspaper distress print*.

#### Master Prompt Specification:
- **Generation Prompt:**
  > `A dark satirical 19th-century engraving of a classical philosopher's bust seen from behind, with an open geometric square doorway in the cranium and a crow perched on top. High contrast etching style, dramatic cross-hatching, isolated on plain white background, flat graphic artwork for screenprint, no background shading, crisp silhouettes.`
