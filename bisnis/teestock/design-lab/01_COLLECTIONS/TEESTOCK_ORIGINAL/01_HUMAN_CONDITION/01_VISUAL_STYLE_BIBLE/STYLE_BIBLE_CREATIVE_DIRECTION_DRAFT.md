# 1. COLLECTION ESSENCE

### Core Idea
Human beings are intelligent, irrational, contradictory, self-deceptive, and often unaware of their own absurdity.  
HUMAN CONDITION explores the strange relationship between human intelligence, behavior, ego, morality, society, and reality.

### Collection Philosophy
> *"An observation of the human species, made by one of its own."*

Collection ini mengamati manusia, bukan berdiri seolah-olah berada di luar kemanusiaan. Humor boleh tajam. Kritik boleh keras. Tetapi sudut pandangnya tetap reflektif, cerdas, dan memiliki kesadaran diri.

### Core Themes
- Human stupidity and intellectual arrogance.
- Hypocrisy and double standards.
- Ego, vanity, and self-deception.
- Manipulation and social conditioning.
- Absurdity of modern society.
- Intelligence versus wisdom.
- Human contradictions.
- The illusion of control.
- Ignorance disguised as certainty.
- The uncomfortable relationship between truth and perception.

### Brand Positioning
HUMAN CONDITION adalah collection graphic apparel yang menggabungkan:
- Intellectual commentary.
- Dark and dry humor.
- Philosophical observations.
- Editorial graphic design.
- Symbolic and conceptual illustration.

*Bukan motivational apparel. Bukan merchandise meme. Bukan sekadar quote collection.*

# 4. VISUAL LANGUAGE (3 TERRITORIES)

HUMAN CONDITION menggunakan tiga visual territories utama:

### A. EDITORIAL ARCHIVE
- **Role:** Membawa kesan intelektual, historis, dan observasional.
- **Visual Characteristics:** Vintage editorial illustration, old newspaper and archival aesthetics, classic serif typography, editorial poster composition, engraving and etched details, structured layouts, controlled imperfections.
- **Suitable Concepts:** Human intelligence, social hypocrisy, intellectual arrogance, cultural criticism.

### B. DARK SATIRICAL ENGRAVING
- **Role:** Menyampaikan kritik manusia melalui humor visual yang tajam.
- **Visual Characteristics:** Expressive engraved illustration, exaggerated human expressions, symbolic objects, dark humor, dense cross-hatching, dramatic light and shadow, distressed print texture.
- **Suitable Concepts:** Stupidity, ego, manipulation, vanity, human absurdity.

### C. PHILOSOPHICAL SURREALISM
- **Role:** Menyampaikan gagasan abstrak melalui visual metaphor.
- **Visual Characteristics:** Surreal conceptual imagery, unexpected object relationships, distorted proportions, symbolic compositions, strong negative space, minimal but meaningful ornament, thought-provoking visual paradoxes.
- **Suitable Concepts:** Reality and perception, self-deception, the illusion of control, intelligence versus wisdom, human contradictions.

> **Consistency Rule:** Ketiga territories boleh berbeda dalam pendekatan, tetapi harus tetap memiliki DNA yang sama: *intellectual commentary, purposeful symbolism, restrained palette, strong composition, and deliberate typography.*

### Color Rules
- Black and aged ivory establish the dominant identity.
- Accent colors are used selectively.
- Avoid unnecessary rainbow palettes and excessive digital gradients.
- Maintain contrast against intended garment colors.
- Do not force every design to use every palette color.

### Garment Compatibility
Prioritas eksplorasi awal: Black, Washed Black, Natural / Off-white, Vintage Grey, Muted Earth Tones.

# 6. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

- **Primary Direction:** Classic editorial serif (strong contrast, expressive letterforms, intellectual authority).
- **Secondary Direction:** Condensed grotesque sans-serif (compact, bold, direct headline presence).
- **Supporting Categories:** Monospaced, small editorial serif, typewriter-inspired lettering.
- **Philosophy:** Typography is not merely decoration; it is part of the message. Typography must follow the concept.
- **Rule:** Do not force every design into a quote layout. Final typography must be manually verified and composed in an editable design environment (Inkscape/Photopea).

---

# 7. ILLUSTRATION SYSTEM

- **Preferred Techniques:** Engraving, etching, cross-hatching, vintage editorial illustration, woodcut-inspired graphic treatment, conceptual surreal illustration, symbolic line art.
- **Characteristics:** Strong silhouette, intentional line weight, expressive details, controlled imperfections, readable at garment viewing distance.
- **Subject Treatment:** Human subjects may be realistic, exaggerated, symbolic, anatomically distorted, or represented through objects/metaphors.
- **Prohibited / Avoid:** **Avoid making every design depend on skulls, brains, eyeballs, or generic vintage illustrations.** Symbols must have conceptual reasons to exist.

---

# 8. COMPOSITION SYSTEM (5 FAMILIES)

1. `01 — Editorial Statement`: Typography-led with supporting illustration.
2. `02 — Central Illustration`: One dominant visual metaphor with restrained supporting text.
3. `03 — Archive Poster`: Structured headline, illustration, secondary info, and editorial hierarchy.
4. `04 — Conceptual Minimalism`: One unusual symbol or object relationship with generous negative space.
5. `05 — Experimental Collage`: Multiple visual elements arranged into an intentional conceptual composition.

*Rule:* Composition should vary between designs. Do not let five designs become the same poster template with different quotations.

---

# 9. TEXTURE & FINISHING

- **Preferred:** Distressed ink, vintage print grain, engraving lines, halftone, subtle paper-like imperfections, controlled screen-print aesthetic.
- **Restrictions:** Texture must not obscure text. Avoid excessive random noise or artificial aging that makes artwork look cheap.
- **Principle:** Texture should support the concept and collection identity, not be used to disguise weak artwork.

---

# 10. CREATIVE SIGNATURE

Every HUMAN CONDITION design should contain at least two of the following:
1. Strong intellectual or social observation.
2. Unexpected visual metaphor.
3. Meaningful irony.
4. Distinctive editorial typography.
5. Symbolic illustration.
6. Deliberate conceptual contradiction.

---

# 11. LOCKED VS FLEXIBLE ELEMENTS

### LOCKED
- Intellectual, cynical, provocative personality.
- Wearable social commentary positioning.
- Purposeful conceptual visual language.
- Restrained, vintage-influenced palette.
- Editorial typography philosophy.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- High-quality illustration and finishing.
- Production-aware design.

### FLEXIBLE
- Subject matter.
- Specific color combinations.
- Illustration technique.
- Composition family.
- Typography arrangement.
- Density of visual details.
- Degree of surrealism.
- Presence or absence of supporting text.

---

# 12. AVOID LIST

HUMAN CONDITION should strictly avoid:
- Generic motivational graphics.
- Generic quote-only layouts.
- Random skull and brain imagery.
- Unnecessary grunge.
- Overused internet meme aesthetics.
- Unrelated decorative elements.
- Excessively complicated compositions.
- AI-looking anatomy and plastic digital illustration.
- Incorrect or distorted typography.
- Direct imitation of another artist's recognizable work.
- Designs that depend on a trend but have no lasting concept.

---

# 13. COLLECTION QUALITY TEST

Before approving an artwork, ask:
1. Does the idea say something meaningful about human behavior?
2. Is the message distinctive?
3. Does the visual metaphor add value?
4. Does the composition have a clear focal point?
5. Does the typography strengthen the idea?
6. Does it belong to HUMAN CONDITION?
7. Would someone wear it without needing to explain the entire concept?
8. Does it avoid looking like generic AI merchandise?
9. Can it be produced with acceptable quality?
10. Does it have a reason to exist beyond being visually attractive?
