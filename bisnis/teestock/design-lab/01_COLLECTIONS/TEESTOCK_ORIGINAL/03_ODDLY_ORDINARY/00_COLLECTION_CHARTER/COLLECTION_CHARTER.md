- **Visual Direction:** Character illustration, hand-drawn quirky graphics, retro comic strip, mid-century advertising parody, playful warm typography.
- **Color Palette:** #22201D (Coffee Charcoal), #FFF8E7 (Warm Milk/Vanilla), #F97316 (Muted Tangerine), #0EA5E9 (Retro Cyan)
